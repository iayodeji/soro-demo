# Sóró Brand Guide

This is the source of truth for Sóró’s visual and verbal identity.

## Brand Positioning

Sóró is a verified student-insights platform. It connects companies to real Nigerian university students through WhatsApp-led conversations, then turns those conversations into useful research.

The brand promise is:

- verified voices
- honest answers
- fast turnaround
- real cash for students

## Core Identity

The Sóró mark represents voice turned into insight. The product should feel culturally grounded, direct, trustworthy, and professional—not decorative, overly corporate, or generic AI software.

## Color System

Sóró uses a deliberately limited interface palette: black, white, and one signal color. This gives the product the clarity of a serious work tool while the Sóró mark retains its cultural character.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas / surface | `#FFFFFF` | Default page and card background |
| Ink / primary text | `#111111` | Headings, body text, strong borders, dark actions |
| Secondary text | `#666666` | Supporting copy and inactive states |
| Subtle surface | `#F5F5F5` | Quiet separation and hover surfaces |
| Border | `#E5E5E5` | Dividers and input outlines |
| Signal orange | `#F24E1E` | Primary action, active state, key emphasis |
| Signal orange text | `#111111` | Text and icons placed on signal orange |

### Color rule

At first glance, every screen should look black and white. Signal orange earns attention only when it communicates the next action or an active state. Do not use gradients, tinted canvases, decorative color blobs, or additional UI accent colors.

The logo artwork retains its own internal colors; they do not expand the product UI palette. Use `soro-logo-mark.jpg` for favicons and compact marks, and `soro-logo-horizontal.jpg` for headers, navigation, and other wide placements.

## Typography

Inter is the single interface typeface.

- Headings: Inter, 600–800 weight
- Body: Inter, 400–500 weight
- Data and microcopy: Inter, 500–600 weight; use tabular figures when available

Headlines are plainspoken, concise, and confident. Avoid display serifs, all-caps labels, artificial letter spacing, and decorative type treatments. Use size contrast and whitespace to establish hierarchy.

## Voice and Tone

Sóró speaks like a sharp, trustworthy operator: clear, human, grounded, confident, and locally aware.

- Say what the product does plainly.
- Prefer trust, proof, verification, and speed over vague hype.
- Do not use words such as “revolutionary” or “game-changing.”
- Keep student-facing language respectful and straightforward; never lean into gig-work slang.

Useful phrase territory: verified students, honest answers, real talk, cash—not points, fast research, actionable insight.

## UI Principles

### Layout

- The public entry page is one viewport: no stacked marketing sections and no scroll-dependent story.
- Every page has one job and one clear primary action.
- Company pages are desktop-first, using conventional sidebar and workspace patterns when a dashboard is introduced.
- Student journeys should feel conversational and WhatsApp-native, not like a compressed dashboard.
- Use clean borders, square-to-gently-rounded corners, and little to no elevation.

### Motion and imagery

- Use movement only when it helps explain a state change.
- Let real imagery, product evidence, or the Sóró logo supply personality when needed.
- Avoid grain, glassmorphism, floating dots, glow effects, and generic AI illustrations.

## Do

- Use white backgrounds with near-black text.
- Use Inter throughout the interface.
- Use signal orange for the single primary action or active state.
- Keep the product message centred on verified human insight.
- Make trust visible through structure, evidence, and clarity.

## Don’t

- Use cream as the main canvas or introduce multiple accent colors into UI.
- Use gradients, blobs, or generated-looking decorative graphics.
- Make the company experience feel like a generic fintech dashboard.
- Add sections merely to make a page feel longer.
- Lose research credibility in pursuit of trend-driven styling.

## Design North Star

Sóró is a clear, trusted bridge between companies seeking answers and students whose voices matter: direct, credible, and unmistakably human.
